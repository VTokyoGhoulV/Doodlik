Output ONLY the astronaut HELMET ACCESSORY from ref 2, in same position and proportions relative to ref 1 cat, on a completely transparent canvas. The accessory consists of a thin fitted open-face white visor frame with hand-drawn black outside outline and narrow sky blue inside line: curved brow below exposed ear tips, cheek-following sides, curved chin rim, two tiny white-orange side pods. No cat, no face, no yellow/orange fur, no ears, no body, no suit, no tail, no other pixels; center of visor must be fully TRANSPARENT to allow any cat face/color to show through. Keep visual design and loose black crayon lines of ref 2, not pixel art. Center overlay on canvas and leave broad transparent margin matching full-body cat canvas so Unity can overlay the whole texture at exactly the original cat's full sprite size. Carefully ensure rim starts below the pointy ears; ears must not be covered or duplicated. Helmet accessory should be beautiful and precisely fitted.
